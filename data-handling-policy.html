Data Handling Policy

PM SHRI Chennai · 27–29 October 2026

Edutrip processes participant details securely for this programme’s travel coordination, accommodation, meals, accessibility support, ID cards, venue entry and emergency response. Under the government project instructions, participants are asked to provide a masked Aadhaar copy. Conceal the first eight digits and QR code before uploading; Edutrip does not request an unmasked Aadhaar number or copy.

Private access and use

Registration records, passport photographs and masked Aadhaar copies are stored in private storage. Access to Aadhaar copies is restricted to the designated founder account. Other registration details are available to authorised operations staff. Necessary details may be shared with authorised travel and accommodation providers, host institutions and emergency responders. Medical declarations are used for support, planning and emergencies. Aadhaar copies are used only for flight tickets and venue entry and are not included in general airline exports.

30-day retention

The retention period starts after the trip concludes on 29 October 2026. Details, photos and masked Aadhaar copies are retained through 28 November 2026. Scheduled removal from the active registration database and private file storage begins on 29 November 2026 (India time), with retries if a cleanup attempt fails. This includes duplicate records removed from the active list.

Provider-held copies and platform backups may follow their own retention schedules; the automated removal described here covers Edutrip’s active registration database and private storage. Staff should remove programme exports and working copies at the same deadline.

Corrections and requests

Contact Edutrip on 7989054712 for corrections or deletion requests. Please quote your registration reference. Possible duplicates are reviewed by staff; no duplicate is automatically removed before review.
