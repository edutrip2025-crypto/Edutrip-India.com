Data Handling Policy

PM SHRI Chennai · 28–31 October 2026

Edutrip processes participant details securely for this programme’s travel coordination, accommodation, meals, accessibility support, ID cards, venue entry and emergency response. We collect the Aadhaar-format name, not an Aadhaar number or copy.

Private access and use

Registration records and passport photographs are stored privately. Access is restricted to authorised operations staff and administrators. Necessary details may be shared with authorised travel and accommodation providers, host institutions and emergency responders. Medical declarations are used for support, planning and emergencies.

30-day retention

The retention period starts after the trip concludes on 31 October 2026. Details and photos are retained through 30 November 2026. Scheduled removal from the active registration database and photo storage begins on 1 December 2026 (India time), with retries if a cleanup attempt fails. This includes duplicate records removed from the active list.

Provider-held copies and platform backups may follow their own retention schedules; the automated removal described here covers Edutrip’s active registration database and photo storage. Staff should remove programme exports and working copies at the same deadline.

Corrections and requests

Contact Edutrip on 7989054712 for corrections or deletion requests. Please quote your registration reference. Possible duplicates are reviewed by staff; no duplicate is automatically removed before review.
